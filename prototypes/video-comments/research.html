A familiar player. A conversation around moments.

There are good React video players. The complete Loom-style conversation layer still needs application code. The selected sidebar prototype explores that layer with real local playback and sample conversations.

Option Finding
Video.js React Recommended library candidate for playback and custom controls. Comments and reactions remain custom.
ReactPlayer A good multi-provider player. No built-in Loom conversation UI.
Annotation Comments Timed comments and replies, but targets Video.js 7; no documented Loom-style emoji reactions.
Vidstack Now in security-only maintenance; its team directs new projects to Video.js 10.

What comes from Loom

The side conversation rail, purple timestamps, timeline markers and emoji capsule follow Loom’s documented interactions and this published screenshot. The wider layout and phone adaptation are proposals. This is not a pixel-certified reproduction of today’s signed-in Loom app.

Try the important moments

  1. Play the video, then tap an emoji. Look for its timeline marker.
  2. Click a comment timestamp to jump back to that moment.
  3. Start a comment, seek elsewhere, then post. Its original time stays attached.
  4. Click the timestamp chip to comment on the whole video.
  5. Try a reply or open the empty state.

What this preview does

Video.js React 10.0.1 now powers the selected sidebar prototype. Everything stays in this browser session. A local video is never uploaded. Emoji animations happen when entered; replay animation and crowded-marker grouping remain future work.

Full research, source links, launch instructions and production considerations are in docs/video-conversation-prototypes.md.
