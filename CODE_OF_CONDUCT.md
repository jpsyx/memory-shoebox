# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Famgram exists so families can share private moments with the people they
trust. The project around it should feel the same way: welcoming, patient, and
safe for everyone who takes part, whatever their background, identity, or level
of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that other people's priorities and constraints differ from yours.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to decline a change. Famgram is deliberately
  small, and "no" is often a product decision rather than a judgment of you.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

Given what this software is for, one thing is worth stating plainly: content or
conduct that sexualizes minors, or that targets a child, results in an
immediate and permanent ban and is reported to the appropriate authorities.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every project space, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any private correspondence about the project. It
also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting on this
repository, or by contacting Sunmiento LLC through
[sunmiento.com](https://sunmiento.com). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose
behavior they judge to be in breach of this document. They will explain the
decision where doing so does not expose the reporter.
